# CivicRule AI privacy notice

Effective 13 September 2026. This notice describes the current development release.

## Information on your device

Business profiles, checklist notes, permits, saved answers and imported documents are stored in the app's local sandbox. Document text recognition runs on device. Voice transcription requires on-device speech support and does not fall back to cloud audio processing. Permission is requested when you choose to record or scan.

## External services

If you deliberately search an address, your search is processed by Apple Maps under Apple's privacy terms. If you configure a CivicRule source service and enable question sharing, the question, nation, authority, business type and activities are sent to that service over HTTPS. Do not include confidential information in a question unless you intend to share it with the configured service. No source service is bundled or active by default in this release.

The included pilot server processes questions to return evidence and does not persist question bodies. It stores public regulatory source snapshots. A deployed service must disclose any additional retention practices before use.

Apple handles any App Store purchase and restore requests. Subscriptions are not active in this release. The app contains no advertising or analytics SDK and does not sell business data.

## Your controls

Use Settings to revoke question-sharing consent, enable device authentication, export records or delete local data. Individual documents, permits, checklist items and saved answers can be deleted. Deleting a business removes associated local records and pending reminders. Exports and operating-system backups you retain are separate copies and are not deleted by the app. There is no cloud account in this release.

The API access credential is stored in the device Keychain. Device authentication is an additional app access control, not a promise of independent file encryption.

## Contact

For support and privacy questions, use the [CivicRule support page](https://github.com/lanray07/CivicRule-AI/issues). Do not post private documents, access tokens, full addresses or other sensitive business information in a public issue. Describe the problem without including those details.
