# Store asset production

Artwork generated with the built-in Imagegen tool, not the API/CLI fallback. Generated originals are retained. The subscription PNG exports are resampled to Apple's 1024 × 1024 RGB specification without creative changes. They contain no prices, unsupported feature claims or readable legal documents. They are promotional artwork, not App Review screenshots or evidence that subscriptions are active.

## Assets

- `CivicRule/Assets.xcassets/ShopOwner.imageset/shop-owner.png`: fictional shop owner inspecting premises; onboarding screen 1.
- `CivicRule/Assets.xcassets/CafeOwner.imageset/cafe-owner.png`: fictional café owner speaking a question; onboarding voice screen.
- `subscriptions/monthly-original.png`, `subscriptions/monthly-1024.png`: single-owner planning still life.
- `subscriptions/annual-original.png`, `subscriptions/annual-1024.png`: two owners planning together.
- Device screenshots are produced in GitHub Actions and downloaded separately; no regulatory answer or monitoring status is fabricated for marketing.

## Final image prompts

### Café voice scene

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: premium iOS business app editorial photo, square 1024 x 1024 pixels. Create a beautifully lit realistic candid photograph of a British South Asian woman café owner in her early 30s, in an olive linen apron over a cream shirt, standing at an oak counter in her small independent café before opening. She holds a smartphone naturally and is speaking a question into it while looking thoughtfully toward the doorway. Tasteful forest green cabinetry, warm plaster, brass fixtures, natural morning light, real coffee equipment softly blurred behind her. Authentic grounded optimism, independent-business magazine art direction. Medium-wide framing with subject in center, room for cropping. Not corporate stock-photo posing. No text, no logos, no icon overlays, no UI, no watermarks. Opaque full-bleed photo, exactly square.
